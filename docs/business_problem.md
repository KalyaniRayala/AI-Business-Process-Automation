# AI Business Process Discovery & Automation Platform

## 1. Business Domain

Finance

## 2. Business Process

Expense Reimbursement

## 3. Business Problem

Finance teams process a large number of employee
expense claims. The process contains repetitive
manual tasks such as receipt verification, policy
checking, employee verification, budget checking,
accounting updates, and employee notifications.

The goal is to identify which tasks can be safely
automated using AI, APIs, databases, and workflows
while keeping human approval for decisions that
require human judgment.

## 4. Current Process

1. Employee submits expense claim.
2. Finance receives the claim.
3. Finance checks the receipt.
4. Finance checks company expense policy.
5. Finance verifies employee details.
6. Finance checks department budget.
7. Manager reviews and approves.
8. Finance performs final approval.
9. Finance updates the accounting system.
10. Employee receives notification.

## 5. Proposed AI Platform

The platform will:

1. Understand the business process.
2. Extract individual process tasks.
3. Identify process bottlenecks.
4. Retrieve relevant company policies.
5. Determine automation opportunities.
6. Select appropriate AI/API/workflow solutions.
7. Generate an automation proposal.
8. Request human approval when required.
9. Execute approved actions.
10. Evaluate the result.
11. Measure ROI and business KPIs.

## 6. Initial Automation Opportunities

- Receipt verification
- Policy verification
- Employee verification
- Budget verification
- Accounting system update
- Employee notification

## 7. Human-Controlled Activities

- Manager approval
- Finance approval
- High-risk decisions
- Exceptions requiring human judgment

## 8. Expected Business Benefits

- Reduce repetitive manual work
- Reduce processing time
- Reduce data-entry errors
- Improve process visibility
- Improve consistency
- Measure automation impact
