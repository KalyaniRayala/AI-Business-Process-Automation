Expense Reimbursement Process

1. Employee submits an expense claim along with the receipt.

2. The finance team receives the expense claim.

3. Finance checks whether the receipt is valid and contains the required information.

4. Finance checks the company expense reimbursement policy.

5. Finance verifies the employee's identity and department.

6. Finance checks whether sufficient department budget is available.

7. Expenses above $500 require manager approval.

8. Expenses above $5,000 require finance approval.

9. Approved expenses are recorded in the accounting system.

10. The employee receives an email notification about the final decision.
